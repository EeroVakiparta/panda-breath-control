## Use at your own risk

The program switches a 300 W mains-powered heater on and off and sets its
temperature. By using it you accept that:

- Heated prints should be supervised. If you leave a print or the heater
  running unattended, that is your decision and your responsibility. Follow
  your printer maker's guidance and any rules that apply to your home or
  workplace (for example fire safety rules or insurance terms).
- The auto-off timer is a convenience, not a safety device. It works only while
  the program is running and the computer is on and connected to the same
  network as the heater. A crash, power cut, network fault or sleeping
  computer can stop it from switching the heater off.
- Many printers were never designed for a heated chamber. Motors, belts,
  electronics, plastic parts and wiring inside the enclosure may not tolerate
  the temperature you choose. The printer profiles and material presets in
  this program are starting points taken from public sources, not tested
  limits for your printer. Check them yourself.
- Using the heater on a printer its maker does not support may void
  warranties for the printer or the heater.
- The heater's own temperature sensor usually reads higher than the real
  chamber temperature.
- Printing materials such as ABS and ASA give off fumes. A heated chamber with
  a HEPA + carbon filter reduces but does not remove them. Ventilate.
